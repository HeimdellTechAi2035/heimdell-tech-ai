Before you book

Can you physically test Ethernet cables during a remote MOT?

No. Remote diagnostics can help investigate connectivity, but cannot physically wiremap cables, inspect terminations or verify electrical PoE measurements without suitable on-site equipment. Physical testing requires a separately arranged service.

Do you offer Wazuh monitoring through Mary?

In development: optional Wazuh-based device monitoring with a Mary incident dashboard and severity-based email, SMS and automated voice escalation. Pilot availability and pricing are confirmed individually; these features are not yet generally available. After an authorised baseline MOT, eligible pilot devices would be enrolled and tested before monitoring is activated. A computer must be powered on and connected to report; offline devices have a separate status.

Will Mary text or call me about security issues?

In development: optional Wazuh-based device monitoring with a Mary incident dashboard and severity-based email, SMS and automated voice escalation. Pilot availability and pricing are confirmed individually; these features are not yet generally available. The planned policy groups duplicate findings and reserves SMS or voice escalation for qualifying urgent incidents and agreed contacts. Routine issues appear in summaries. A vulnerability does not prove a breach. Detailed evidence belongs in the authenticated portal; calls never request passwords.

Does 24/7 monitoring include 24/7 human incident response?

No. Planned automated monitoring and notification can operate around the clock when enrolled devices and services are online. Human support hours, response arrangements and out-of-hours escalation must be agreed in writing. No 24/7 staffed incident-response service is currently promised.

Does Wazuh automatically update all my software?

Do not assume this. Detection and patching are separate. Any update service needs defined supported software, authorisation, maintenance windows, restart arrangements, failure handling and verification. The planned subscription is scoped individually; no automatic repair of every vulnerability is promised.

What access and permissions are needed for remote checks?

We agree the devices, authorised access method and diagnostic data before starting. Monitoring enrolment requires separate permission and a written subscription. Customer data access is restricted by business; retention and offboarding are agreed. Never email passwords or share them with an automated caller.
