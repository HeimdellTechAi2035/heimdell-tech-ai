What is a Business Technology MOT?

Heimdell visits Preston and Lancashire businesses to test agreed Ethernet cabling, Windows computers, business systems and networks, review security and backups, and deliver a written report. Introductory Essential MOTs cost £99 for the first 10 eligible local bookings; standard Essential £149, Plus £249, Premium £349. Optional repairs start from £65/hour plus parts; tailored monthly IT support starts at £79/month. Repairs and support are not included in the MOT fee.

All checks require authorisation and are not penetration tests or formal certifications. Scope, travel, availability and any applicable VAT are confirmed before booking.

What we test during your MOT

The £99 introductory Essential visit covers one site and up to five agreed Windows computers, typically in 60–90 minutes. The list below shows the full range of MOT checks. We agree the checks, devices and areas to cover before booking. Plus and Premium allow a wider review.

Ethernet cabling and wire testing

Wiremap testing on selected Ethernet patch leads and accessible cable runs to identify wiring faults. We also check for damaged leads, loose connections and suspect terminations.

Network ports, switches and PoE

Check link status and negotiated speed on selected ports, identify switch ports where possible and verify Power over Ethernet where used.

Internet and router testing

Measure upload and download speed, latency and packet loss where relevant. Check gateway reachability, automatic IP assignment (DHCP) and DNS resolution.

Wi-Fi coverage and configuration

Check signal and reliability in agreed work areas. Identify dead spots and signs of interference, review wireless security settings and check whether guest access is separate.

Windows system testing

Check Windows support and updates, CPU and memory usage, responsiveness, storage and drive-health indicators. Review startup problems and recent crashes where relevant.

Business system testing

Check that agreed email, Microsoft 365, tills and other core applications open and can be accessed. Review visible licensing issues, remote access and file-sharing settings where agreed.

Cyber security checks

Review antivirus, firewall, multi-factor authentication, screen locks, administrator permissions, encryption and application updates. Check router firmware and admin settings where authorised.

Backups and recovery readiness

Check what is backed up, the latest job result and evidence that a restore has worked. Live restore tests require separate approval.

CCTV connectivity and recording

Check whether agreed cameras and recorders are online, whether recording indicators show activity and whether there are visible recording gaps.

Printers, scanners and connected equipment

Check agreed equipment connects and responds. Run a test print or other functional check where appropriate.

Equipment and business continuity

Record key equipment, flag ageing or unsupported devices and review how the business would keep working if internet, power or critical equipment failed.

AI and automation opportunities

Identify repetitive work or workflow problems and record where AI or automation could help, the likely benefit and the next step.

Your written report and repair options

Your written report records what we tested, what needs attention, what we couldn’t verify and what to fix first. If you approve a separate repair quote, we can carry out the work and retest the affected system.

Common questions

Before you book

How long does a Technology MOT take?

For a small business, an Essential inspection is designed around a 60–90 minute visit. Larger or more complex sites may require a longer appointment and a revised quotation.

Will you need passwords or access to our computers?

We agree access and authorisation in advance. Some checks need a staff member to sign in or supervise access. We will not ask you to email passwords or make changes without permission.

Is this a formal cybersecurity certificate or penetration test?

No. It is a practical technology health check and basic cybersecurity-hygiene review, not a penetration test, Cyber Essentials certification, statutory inspection or guarantee that systems are free of vulnerabilities.

What if you identify a problem?

We document the finding and recommend next steps. If it is a fault we can safely address, we can give you a separate quote. You decide whether any repair goes ahead.

Does the £79/month IT plan include unlimited repairs and callouts?

No. Monthly plans start from £79 with coverage agreed in writing. Extra on-site visits, repair labour, replacement parts, licences and out-of-hours assistance are not included unless specified in your agreement.

Do you only work with offices?

No. We aim to support shops, cafés, salons, professional services, property businesses, warehouses and other small organisations in Preston and the surrounding Lancashire area.
