Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol implementation

Let AI agents discover, query and book your business

Quick answer: The Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol is an open standard, launched by Google in 2025 and now hosted by the Linux Foundation, that lets AI agents discover what another agent or business can do and send it tasks. Implementing A2A means a customer’s AI assistant can ask your business for availability, a quote or a booking directly, instead of scraping your website.

What we build

  • Agent Card: a machine-readable description of your business’s capabilities (for example “check availability”, “request quote”, “book appointment”), published on your domain.
  • A2A endpoint: a secure service that receives agent requests and connects them to your existing booking system, CRM, calendar or enquiry inbox.
  • Guardrails: what agents may and may not do, rate limits, human approval steps where you want them, and logging of every request.
  • Verified consent (optional): an independent record of what the end customer agreed to, using our verified-consent platform.
