Get your business ready for AI agents

AI agent readiness, A2A and AP2 implementation for UK businesses

Quick answer: AI agents (assistants that act on a customer's behalf) are starting to search for, compare, book and pay for services. To be chosen, a business needs to be readable by agents, reachable by them through an open standard like Google's Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol, and able to accept agent-initiated payments safely through the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2). Heimdell Tech Ai is a Preston-based software company that implements all three for UK businesses.

What changes when customers use AI agents

Today a customer searches, reads your website and fills in a form. With an agent, the customer says “book me a boiler service for Saturday morning”, and the agent does the rest. If it can’t understand your services, check your availability or complete a booking, it moves on to a business it can.

The three layers we implement

  1. Agent-readable website: clean structure, schema.org markup, clear prices and an llms.txt file, so AI systems describe your business accurately. See website upgrades.
  2. A2A connection: an Agent Card that tells AI agents what your business can do, connected to your real booking, quoting or enquiry system. See A2A implementation.
  3. AP2 payments and proof of consent: agents can pay you on a customer’s behalf with a signed record of what the customer authorised. Our verified-consent platform adds an independent record of every customer agreement. See AP2 agent payments.

Who it’s for

UK businesses that take bookings, quotes or orders: trades, clinics, salons, gyms, hospitality, retailers and service companies.
