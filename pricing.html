Pricing FAQs

How much do the AI agent and website services cost?

The free agent-readiness scan costs nothing. An AI agent readiness review starts from £500. Implementing a standard starts from £1,500 (UCP readiness, Knowledge Catalog readiness), £2,000 (MCP server), £2,500 (A2A) or £4,000 (AP2). Website upgrades start from £1,500. Every project is scoped and quoted individually after a free call.

How much does TPV verification cost?

TPV phone verification costs from 80p per call, paid as credits with no subscription or setup fee. A phone verification uses 5 credits; credit packs run from £20 for 100 credits (£1.00 per call) down to £320 for 2,000 credits (80p per call). Link verification costs 1 credit.

Is there a monthly fee or subscription?

No. TPV verification is pay-as-you-go — you buy a credit pack and use it whenever you need it, with no monthly minimum, no subscription and no setup fee.

Is the commission clawback audit really free?

Yes. The 15-minute commission clawback audit is free, with no obligation to purchase anything afterwards.

How is compliance protocol setup and implementation priced?

Compliance protocol setup starts from £1,500 as a one-off project fee. A larger, end-to-end compliance protocol implementation — covering CRM integration and automated evidence trails — starts from £3,000. Exact pricing depends on scope and is confirmed after a free audit call.

Do you charge for high-volume or channel partner programmes?

High-volume partner and channel/vendor compliance programmes are quoted individually and typically run on the same credit model at bulk rates from £0.16 per credit, adjusted for volume and network size.
