Website upgrades for search, AI answers and AI agents

Be found, described accurately and chosen

Quick answer: A website upgrade from Heimdell Tech Ai makes your existing site fast, structured and easy for Google, AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) and AI agents to understand, so you’re found, described accurately and chosen.

What’s included

  • Technical SEO: titles, meta descriptions, headings, canonical tags, sitemap and robots.txt.
  • Structured data: Organization/LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ and Product markup, with your real details.
  • AI-readiness: an llms.txt file, direct-answer content and AI crawler access.
  • Speed: image compression, lean code and good mobile scores.
  • Clear services and prices: the information agents and customers need to decide.
  • Optional: A2A and AP2 integration.
