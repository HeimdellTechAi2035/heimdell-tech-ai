UCP for UK Shopify and WooCommerce Shops

What the platforms publish, and what your product data still has to say

Quick answer: UCP is a way for an agent to read a catalogue and check out. UK shops on Shopify or WooCommerce still need clean product data, delivery and returns in text, and, if an agent will pay, a payment authorisation such as AP2. Shopify publishes a UCP profile on storefronts. WooCommerce’s own pages describe UCP as planned or piloted, not as a switch every UK shop can already use. Heimdell Tech Ai does not sell a Shopify or WooCommerce app. The readiness work is a custom quote.

Same protocol, different platforms

The UCP specification says a business publishes a profile at /.well-known/ucp. The profile states a protocol version, services, optional capabilities and payment handlers. A shopping agent fetches it and the two sides settle on a shared set of capabilities. Google’s 11 January 2026 announcement described UCP as an open standard co-developed with industry leaders including Shopify, and compatible with AP2.

That is the protocol. A platform then decides whether it writes the profile for you, which version it advertises, and whether checkout is actually open in the United Kingdom. Those decisions change. The notes below are what the official pages said when this guide was written, in November 2026. Check them again before you spend money.

The platform-agnostic service is UCP readiness. The checkout-and-payment story is how AP2 and UCP work together.

Shopify: a profile on the storefront

Shopify’s agent-profile documentation says that in UCP the business profile is published by the merchant or the platform, typically at /.well-known/ucp. On Shopify that file is on the merchant storefront, at {shop}.myshopify.com/.well-known/ucp. Shopify describes it as stating the protocol version, services, capabilities, payment handlers and signing keys. The agent sends its own platform profile. Shopify, acting as the business, intersects the two and chooses the active capabilities for the session. If the agent’s profile cannot be loaded or nothing matches, the documentation says you get an error path rather than a successful negotiation.

A Shopify changelog entry dated 4 September 2026 says storefront discovery profiles advertise UCP version 2026-08-25. The entry says the set of capabilities Shopify supports was unchanged by that update, and that existing integrations did not have to change for the version number alone.

What this means for a UK shop, without adding anything Shopify has not said:

  • You may already have a discovery file, because the platform publishes it. Fetch /.well-known/ucp on your own storefront and read the version and capabilities it names.
  • A file that exists is not the same as a catalogue an agent can trust. Prices in images, missing sizes, and a returns policy that is only a banner still fail the agent even when the profile is valid.
  • Payment handlers in the profile are the platform’s declaration. Do not assume a handler name means AP2 mandates are being stored for you. Read AP2 agent payments if the question is proof of what the customer authorised.

There is no Heimdell Tech Ai app in the Shopify theme store, and this guide does not ask you to install one.

WooCommerce: read the current page, not a rumour

WooCommerce’s own site does not say one simple “on” or “off” for UK shops. Three public pages, read together, are the honest picture:

  • The agentic commerce overview lists Universal Commerce Protocol, attributed to Google, as “coming soon”. It says stores that already sync to Google Merchant Center or use structured data have a foundation. It also says agents can already buy on stores that take cards, by using checkout the way a customer does. That card path is not the same thing as a UCP profile.
  • The AI shopping document says it describes a feature planned for WooCommerce 11.4, and that the feature and its availability may change before release. It says compatible assistants can find products and add them to a cart, and that shoppers complete orders through the store’s checkout. It says WooCommerce uses UCP to make the store available to compatible shopping services, and that the store needs to be live and on HTTPS.
  • A WooCommerce post dated 19 November 2026 by its product lead for agentic commerce says Google’s UCP launched in January 2026, that UCP checkout was available in the US to eligible merchants through Google Merchant Center and selected platforms, and that expansion to other countries was planned. The same post says neither UCP nor the separate Agentic Commerce Protocol was available broadly to WooCommerce stores yet, that WooCommerce was piloting UCP checkout with Google and a small group of merchants, and that the plan was to enable UCP on all Woo stores by version 11.4.

A UK shop should not treat a pilot, or a document that warns the feature may change, as a promise that their checkout is live. Look at the WooCommerce version you run, fetch /.well-known/ucp if you want to see whether a profile is there, and read the current document rather than this summary once time has passed. We have not implemented UCP “for WooCommerce” as a productised plugin, and we do not claim a place in anyone’s pilot.

Product data, stock, delivery and returns

Both platforms still depend on text an agent can quote. The readiness work is mostly this list, whether or not a profile file already exists:

  • Product text. Title, a description that matches the goods, price, currency (GBP for a UK shop), and availability. Variants, such as size and colour, need to be data, not only a photograph. An identifier such as a SKU stops an agent matching the wrong variant.
  • Stock. If the page says “in stock” when it is not, the agent will repeat that. The profile cannot correct a lie in the catalogue.
  • Delivery. Cost, method and timing, in sentences. Say which option is the standard one. Distance-selling guidance expects delivery arrangements before the order. The consumer-law detail is in when an AI agent buys.
  • Returns. Who can cancel, how long they have, which items are excluded, and how to send goods back. A policy that is an image will be skipped or guessed.

Website upgrades are the related service when the shop site itself is hard for people and agents to read. Agentic commerce for UK small businesses is the wider order of work: facts first, then a protocol.

Where AP2 fits

UCP’s profile can name payment handlers. AP2’s specification says it is the security layer for an agent payment, designed to work with UCP, and that the commerce messages themselves are outside AP2. If you want a signed mandate that binds a checkout hash to an amount and a payee, that is the AP2 question, not a theme setting. The journey is written out in how AP2 and UCP work together for UK shops. Do not turn on a payment experiment because a discovery file exists.

Frequently asked questions

Does Heimdell Tech Ai have a Shopify or WooCommerce app for UCP?

No. There is no Heimdell app or plugin to install. UCP readiness is a human review of your profile, product data and policies, priced as a custom quote. Platform features, where they exist, are the platform's, and you should read that platform's current documentation.

Do Shopify stores already speak UCP?

Shopify's developer documentation says a shop publishes a business profile at /.well-known/ucp on the storefront. A Shopify changelog dated 4 September 2026 says storefronts advertise UCP version 2026-08-25 in that profile. That is a platform fact about discovery. It does not mean every product, policy and payment path on a UK shop is ready for an agent.

Can WooCommerce take UCP checkouts today?

Treat the status as moving, and read WooCommerce's current pages. Their agentic commerce page lists Universal Commerce Protocol as coming soon. Their AI shopping document describes a feature planned for WooCommerce 11.4, which may change before release, and says shoppers complete orders through the store checkout. A WooCommerce post dated 19 November 2026 says UCP checkout is not available broadly to WooCommerce stores yet, that a pilot is under way, and that the plan is to enable it by version 11.4. Do not assume your shop is in that pilot.

Do I still need AP2 if I use Shopify or WooCommerce?

UCP covers discovery and commerce capabilities such as checkout. AP2 covers a signed mandate for the payment. If an agent will pay, you need an authorisation story as well as a catalogue. See the guide on how AP2 and UCP work together. Whether your platform and payment provider support that path today is a separate check.
