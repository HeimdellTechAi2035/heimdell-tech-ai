Stop Non-Compliant Sales Before Provisioning

Most compliance problems are discovered after the fact—when a customer complains, a provider audits, or a clawback notice arrives. By then, the damage is done.

Sales compliance verification catches issues at the point of sale. Every contract is independently checked before it enters the provider system, creating an opportunity to resolve problems while they're still fixable.

The approach: Independent verification call within 48 hours of sale, checking customer understanding and creating documented compliance evidence before the contract is provisioned.

The Verification Checklist

  • Identity Match: Customer name, address and DOB confirmed against sale record
  • Sale Recall: Customer confirms they spoke with a sales agent and requested the service
  • Service Understanding: Customer can describe what they're signing up for
  • Terms Confirmation: Contract length, pricing and any additional fees verified
  • Rights Disclosure: 14-day cooling-off period and cancellation process explained
  • Consent Recording: Verbal agreement captured with timestamp
  • Documentation: Compliance certificate generated for provider audit

Compliance Verification FAQs

What is telecom sales compliance verification?

Telecom sales compliance verification is a systematic check of every sale against regulatory and provider requirements before contract provisioning. It confirms customer identity, validates consent, verifies terms were properly disclosed, and creates documented evidence for audits. This prevents non-compliant sales from entering the provider system.

Which regulations apply to UK telecom sales?

UK telecom sales must comply with Ofcom General Conditions (especially GC C1 for customer protection), Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) 2003, Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, UK GDPR for data handling, and individual provider compliance requirements. Non-compliance can result in fines, clawbacks and loss of reseller agreements.

How does compliance verification prevent clawbacks?

Compliance verification creates documented proof that each sale followed proper procedures. When customers dispute contracts, providers review this evidence before deciding on clawbacks. Sales with verified consent, recorded disclosures and timestamped documentation are far less likely to result in commission recovery.

What happens to sales that fail verification?

Sales that fail compliance verification are flagged before provisioning. You receive the specific failure reason (identity mismatch, missing consent, terms dispute) and can address the issue with the customer. This early catch prevents complaints, disputes and clawbacks from entering your provider relationship.
