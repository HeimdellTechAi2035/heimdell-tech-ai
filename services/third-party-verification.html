What is Third-Party Verification (TPV)?

TPV is an independent verification process where a trained agent — not involved in the original sale — contacts the customer to confirm they understand and agreed to the contract.

The call is recorded and timestamped, creating legally defensible evidence that the sale was compliant. This protects you from customer disputes, provider audits and commission clawbacks.

Why "third party"?

The verification is performed by someone independent from your sales team. This removes any bias and creates unquestionable proof that the customer confirmed the sale without pressure from the original salesperson.

What We Verify on Every Call

Each TPV call confirms four critical elements that providers require for audit compliance:

👤

Customer Identity

We confirm full name, address and date of birth to verify the correct person is authorising the contract.

📄

Key Contract Terms

We explain the monthly cost, contract length, early termination fees and any special conditions, then confirm understanding.

📅

Cooling-Off Rights

We explain the 14-day cancellation period required under Consumer Contracts Regulations and confirm acknowledgement.

💳

Direct Debit Authority

We confirm the customer authorises the Direct Debit payment and understands the billing schedule.

Why TPV Matters for Telecom Resellers

Without documented proof of customer consent, you're exposed to three major risks:

⚠

Customer Disputes

Customers claim they didn't understand the terms or didn't authorise the sale. Without TPV, it's your word against theirs.

💰

Commission Clawbacks

Providers reverse your commission when sales fail audit. A single disputed sale can cost hundreds or thousands in recovered revenue.

⚖

Regulatory Risk

Ofcom and the ICO require documented consent. PECR fines can reach £17.5M or 4% of turnover. Non-compliant sales expose your business.

The bottom line:

TPV removes the grounds for disputes. When a provider audits your sales or a customer complains, you have recorded proof of exactly what was confirmed. This protects your commissions and keeps you compliant.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is third-party verification (TPV)?

Third-party verification (TPV) is an independent phone call made to customers after a telecom sale to confirm they understand and agreed to the contract. An agent not involved in the original sale verifies identity, explains the key terms, confirms pricing and contract length, and records verbal consent. This creates independent proof the sale was compliant.

What does the TPV call verify?

The TPV call verifies four things: customer identity (name, address, date of birth), understanding of key contract terms (price, contract length, early termination fees), acknowledgement of cooling-off rights (14-day cancellation period), and authorisation of the Direct Debit mandate. Each point is confirmed verbally and recorded.

Why do telecom resellers need TPV?

TPV protects telecom resellers from commission clawbacks when customers dispute contracts. Providers require documented proof of informed consent. Without TPV, a disputed sale can result in full commission recovery plus penalties. TPV creates an independent audit trail that satisfies provider compliance requirements.

What evidence do I receive from TPV?

You receive three things for every verified sale: a voice log recording of the full verification call, a timestamped audit trail showing verification time, agent details and outcome, and a compliance certificate you can present to providers during audits. All records are stored securely and retrievable on demand.

How quickly are TPV calls made after a sale?

TPV calls are typically made within 24 hours of receiving sale details. For high-volume operations, calls are completed within 2-4 hours during business hours. Fast verification reduces the risk of customers forgetting details before confirmation. Status updates are logged directly to your CRM.
