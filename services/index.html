UK-wide remote Business Technology MOT

Firms in Preston, Penwortham and the wider Lancashire area use the same remote check. Business Technology MOT for Preston and Lancashire.

An authorised remote review of agreed Windows computers, performance, updates, available protective settings, business applications and backup evidence, with a human-reviewed report. Account security and business processes are checked with the customer. Findings distinguish measured results, customer answers and unverified areas. Physical cable wiremap testing, hardware repairs and whole-premises Wi-Fi surveys are not included remotely.
