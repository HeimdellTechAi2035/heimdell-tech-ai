Common Questions About SME Compliance

What is the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025?

+
The DUAA 2025 introduces mandatory 30-day response windows for customer data requests. Under this new legislation, any business that collects customer data must respond to Subject Access Requests (SARs) and complaints within 30 days. Miss the deadline and you face ICO investigation — regardless of business size. The Heimdell Protocol automatically tracks all deadlines and handles responses for you, so you never miss a window.

How does The Heimdell Protocol protect my business?

+
It creates documented proof of every customer interaction, removing the grounds for disputes and fines. Customer disputes typically occur when there’s no proof of what was agreed — pricing, terms, consent. The Heimdell Protocol automatically documents every interaction: instant identity verification, automated T&Cs via SMS, and timestamped records. When a dispute or ICO inquiry arrives, you have irrefutable evidence ready.

What happens if the ICO investigates my business?

+
You present compliance documentation that contains all required evidence instantly. When the ICO investigates, they request evidence that you handle customer data properly, respond to complaints on time, and have documented consent. The Heimdell Protocol generates audit-ready documentation for every customer, stored with timestamps and delivery logs. Investigation becomes a straightforward evidence exercise, not a crisis.

How quickly does verification happen?

+
Under 60 seconds — triggered instantly when you agree a job. The Heimdell Protocol activates the moment you confirm a job with a customer. Identity verification happens at the door or on the phone. T&Cs are sent via SMS within seconds. Everything is documented automatically. You work, we handle compliance.

Is The Heimdell Protocol compliant with UK regulations?

+
Yes — built around UK GDPR, PECR 2003, DUAA 2025 and Consumer Contracts Regulations. The Heimdell Protocol is designed around the regulatory framework governing UK businesses, including UK GDPR data protection requirements, PECR 2003 electronic communications rules, the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 complaint handling requirements, and the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 covering cooling-off rights.

Yes — documentation and compliance data integrate with your existing workflow. The Heimdell Protocol is designed to work alongside your current systems. Compliance documentation, timestamps and delivery logs are accessible on demand. No need to change how you work — we add a protection layer on top.

The Heimdell Protocol provides timestamped documentation as permanent proof. If a customer claims they never agreed to the price, never received terms, or “never hired you” — The Heimdell Protocol provides irrefutable evidence to the contrary: timestamped identity verification, SMS delivery logs, and a compliance record summarising every checkpoint. This evidence is indexed and retrievable on demand — serving as permanent defence against retrospective disputes.
