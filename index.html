Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Telecom Verification Calls

What is a telecom verification call (TPV)?

+
A TPV is an independent phone call made after every sale to confirm the customer genuinely agreed to the contract. We verify their identity, explain the key terms including monthly cost and contract length, confirm their cooling-off rights, and record their verbal consent. This call happens separately from your sales team to provide unbiased proof the sale was compliant.

Why do telecom resellers get commission clawbacks?

+
Commission clawbacks happen when providers find gaps in your sales compliance during audits. Common causes include missing consent evidence, customers who claim they didn’t understand the terms, and Direct Debit disputes. Without recorded proof of what the customer agreed to, providers reverse the commission by default.

What does the verification call confirm?

+
The verification call confirms four things: customer identity, understanding of key contract terms, acknowledgement of cooling-off rights, and authorisation of the Direct Debit mandate. Each point is explained clearly and the customer’s verbal confirmation is recorded. This creates a complete audit trail for every sale.

Are verification calls recorded?

+
Yes, every verification call is recorded in full. The recording captures the customer confirming their identity, acknowledging the contract terms, and authorising payment. These recordings are stored securely and can be retrieved instantly if a provider requests evidence during an audit.

What evidence do we get for provider audits?

+
You receive a compliance certificate for every verified sale. This includes the call recording, timestamped verification details, customer identity confirmation, and copies of any SMS or email confirmations sent. This documentation is designed specifically to satisfy Virgin Media, O2, Sky and other major provider audit requirements.

How fast after the sale is the customer contacted?

+
We make verification calls within 24 hours of receiving your sale details. For high-volume operations, calls are typically completed within 2–4 hours during business hours. You receive status updates in your CRM as each verification completes or if any issues arise.

What is telecom sales compliance?

+
Telecom sales compliance means following the rules set by Ofcom, the ICO and UK consumer law when selling broadband, mobile or VoIP contracts. It includes verifying customer identity, explaining cooling-off rights, documenting consent, and keeping records that satisfy provider audits. Without proper compliance, resellers face commission clawbacks and regulatory fines.

How does verification improve commission protection?

+
Verification creates recorded proof that the customer understood and agreed to the contract. When a provider audits your sales or a customer disputes a charge, you have timestamped evidence showing exactly what was confirmed. This removes the grounds for clawbacks because you can prove the sale was compliant.

Do you serve small resellers in Lancashire?

+
Yes. We work with telecom resellers of all sizes across Lancashire and the rest of the UK. Whether you process ten sales a month or ten thousand, our managed TPV service scales to fit. We are based in Preston, Lancashire, and provide the same enterprise-grade verification to small local resellers as we do to national agencies.
