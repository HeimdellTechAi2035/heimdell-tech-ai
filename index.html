What we check

The Essential MOT is designed for small offices, letting agents, shops and other local businesses. We agree the devices and checks with you before we visit.

Internet and Wi-Fi

Connection performance and Wi-Fi signal in agreed areas.

Windows computers

Basic performance, available storage and update status.

Network equipment

Selected wired connections and equipment you rely on.

Backups

Available evidence of recent backups and any restore checks.

Basic security settings

Agreed checks such as device protection and firewall status.

Printers and connected devices

Basic connectivity checks for selected equipment where agreed.

Before you book

What happens after the introductory offer?

The standard Essential MOT is £149. The £99 launch price applies to the first 10 eligible local Essential bookings, subject to availability.

How much do repairs cost?

Optional repairs start at £65 per hour plus approved parts. We confirm the work and price with you before starting.

Can I book if I already have IT support?

Yes. You can share our findings with your existing IT provider and decide who carries out any recommended work.

Tell us how many devices you need checked. We’ll advise which MOT package fits.
